Master product architecture
 Global platform

We will establish Njumeni as a complete international legal technology company, not merely an AI chatbot, legal document generator or online legal research application.

The objective is to build the entire platform from its foundations: infrastructure, legal knowledge, evidence management, AI reasoning, research, document preparation, case management, professional workspaces, institutional services, security, commercial operations and international expansion.

I will carry forward the JuriPro/JurisAI research and architectural decisions, but use Njumeni as the product and development identity.

The architecture must preserve one fundamental principle:

AI assists with legal research, analysis, evidence and reasoning. It does not pretend to be a lawyer, independently exercise legal authority or replace qualified human judgement.

The platform will serve both ordinary individuals and professional legal practitioners, with institutional and enterprise capabilities.

5.5 Critical database relationships

The platform should enforce these relationships:

    Every matter belongs to an authorised workspace.

    Every workspace belongs to a user or organisation.

    Every evidence item belongs to a matter or an explicitly permitted research workspace.

    Every legal finding references its supporting evidence or authority where applicable.

    Every legal authority has a jurisdiction and source provenance.

    Every authority version preserves its effective dates.

    Every argument node can reference facts, evidence and legal authorities.

    Every document version maintains its revision history.

    Every AI workflow belongs to an authorised execution context.

    Every AI execution records the sources it used.

    Every review and approval is attributable to an authorised human.

    Every sensitive operation produces an audit record.

These are foundational integrity rules, not optional application-level conventions.
